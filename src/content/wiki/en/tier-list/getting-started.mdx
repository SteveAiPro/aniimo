Choosing the right character style and weapon archetype is critical for climbing the leaderboard in Aniimo.

## S-Tier: The Definitive Meta

- **Shadow Blade**: Unrivaled burst damage and hyper-mobility. Can blink behind targets to deal guaranteed critical strikes.
- **Solar Tempest**: S-tier area-of-effect elemental coverage, ideal for fast dungeon room clears and raid boss phases.

## A-Tier: Strong & Reliable

- **Iron Sentinel**: High health pool and defensive counter-skills; very forgiving for learning boss patterns.
- **Frost Archer**: Exceptional ranged kiting ability and consistent freeze crowd control on mobs.

## B-Tier: Situational Choices

- **Flame Brawler**: Good early game damage, but requires close positioning that leaves you vulnerable to late-game boss one-shots.
