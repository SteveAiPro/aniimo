World bosses and dungeon guardians in Aniimo offer the highest tier loot, unique cosmetics, and progression unlocks.

## How do you defeat the Sector Guardian boss?

The first major gatekeeper in Aniimo is the **Sector Guardian**:
- **HP Pool**: Approximately 12,000 HP.
- **Phase 1 (100% - 50% HP)**: The boss relies on frontal cone cleaves and ground slams. Circle around the boss clockwise to continuously hit its back.
- **Phase 2 (50% - 0% HP)**: The guardian enrages, summoning homing projectiles and shockwave pulses. Keep your distance during red telegraph circles and jump over ground waves.

## Recommended boss loadouts and builds

- **Mobility First**: Always equip at least one movement or blink skill to escape corner traps.
- **Sustain Potions**: Bring health recovery items and stamina elixirs into the arena before starting the encounter.
