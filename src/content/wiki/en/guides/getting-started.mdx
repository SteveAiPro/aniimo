Welcome to Aniimo! This guide provides new players with a fast, optimal roadmap through the early game on Roblox without wasting valuable stats or skill points.

## How does combat and skill progression work in Aniimo?

Combat in Aniimo revolves around light attacks, skill combinations, and well-timed dash dodges:
- **Light Attack Chain**: Your primary method to build up combat gauge without draining stamina.
- **Special Abilities**: High-impact anime techniques with cooldowns; always weave them between normal strikes.
- **Dash / Perfect Dodge**: Dodging right before an enemy strike triggers a brief slow-motion vulnerability window.

## What is the fastest leveling route for beginners?

Follow this step-by-step progression route during your first three hours:
1. **Tutorial & Starter Quests**: Complete the basic training grounds to immediately unlock level 5.
2. **Bandit Encampments (Levels 5-15)**: Clear repeat bounty contracts in Sector 1 to stockpile currency and basic gear.
3. **First Dungeon Expedition (Level 15+)**: Form a party or tackle the solo mode to acquire your first Rare weapon drops.

## Common beginner mistakes to avoid in Aniimo

- **Spreading Stat Points Thinly**: Do not distribute stat points equally. Specialize in your chosen attack attribute (Melee or Energy) alongside Defense.
- **Ignoring Promo Codes**: Always redeem all active codes early to get free experience multipliers and start with extra currency.
